Pocket Assistant Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Summary

Pocket Assistant does not have accounts, ads or analytics. We do not run a server and we do not receive your data. Your notes, tasks and settings are saved only on your own device.

What stays on your device

  • Notes, tasks, language, voice and emotion settings. They are stored in your browser's local storage and never sent to us.
  • You can delete all of it at any time with the "Erase everything" button, or by clearing the app's site data.
  • Timers run on your device while the app is open.

Voice and microphone

The microphone is used only when you tap "Mic once" or turn on "Hands-free". Turning speech into text is done by the speech recognition service built into your browser or Android system. On Chrome this service is run by Google, so the audio you speak is sent to Google while the microphone is on, under Google's own privacy policy. Pocket Assistant does not record, store or receive that audio. Replies are spoken by your phone's built-in text-to-speech.

If you do not want any audio to leave your device, do not use the microphone. Typing commands works without sending anything anywhere.

Links you choose to open

The assistant can show a link, for example a DuckDuckGo search. Nothing is sent until you tap the link. After that, the website you open has its own privacy policy.

Children

Pocket Assistant is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
